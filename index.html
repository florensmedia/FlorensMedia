01 · How I can help

Independent consultancy for your media decisions.

From the first questions to the final results, I can support a single project or work alongside your team over the longer term.

01

Is your media budget being used as effectively as it could be?

I review the strategy, channel mix and budget allocation to identify what can be improved. You get an independent view of your media decisions, whether you work with an agency or run campaigns in-house.

02

Not sure which communication direction to take?

I use category, consumer, audience and competitive data to define the target audience, the channels that fit and the level of media pressure needed. This analysis becomes the basis for the brief and the media plan.

03

You've received a media plan. Does it meet your objectives?

I assess the choice of channels, target audience, timing, formats and budget allocation. I can compare scenarios and flag risks or opportunities before the plan goes live.

04

The campaign has run. What did it actually deliver?

I analyse delivery, reach, frequency, media pressure and the contribution of each channel. I put the results in a competitive context and, where the data allows, relate them to changes in brand interest or sales.

05

Plenty of data, but no clear picture?

I build analyses and dashboards that bring together media, sales, distribution, digital and research data. They don't just gather the numbers in one place: they show what is happening, where the gaps are and what is worth watching next.

06

Need support coordinating your media activity?

I can work alongside your team on briefs, proposal reviews, agency coordination and implementation follow-up. Support can cover a single key campaign or continue on an ongoing basis.

04 · Meet Nicoleta

A perspective shaped by more than 20 years in media.

Data only becomes useful once you put it in context.

In more than 20 years in media, I have worked on different sides of the industry: research, media agency, media owner and, more recently, client-side. I started out in media research, in TV audience measurement, as part of the team that set up Romania's first TV audience panel. That is where my experience with audience data comes from, and especially with television — the medium I know best.

My experience covers TV, radio, OOH and cinema. In integrated projects, I also consider how these channels work alongside digital. I have worked on brands in FMCG, retail, pharma, services and many other categories. Every project starts from the client's objective and context, not from a standard media formula. Florens Media brings all of this together: strategy, research, analysis and independent advice.
